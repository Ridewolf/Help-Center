# Privacy and Safety Guidelines

The **Privacy** screen (`/privacy`) is a reading screen: the privacy policy and the safety guidelines, presented as two accordion groups.

If a rider asks how to delete their account, send them to **Profile** instead — see [Profile](profile.md). That is the working flow.

## How riders reach it

- From the **side menu** on the map — that drawer is the app's only navigation shell, and there is no bottom tab bar anywhere in the app, so do not send a rider looking for a tab
- From an operator chat message carrying the privacy app link

**Settings** does **not** link to this screen. Its **Privacy** card carries the two sharing toggles, a **Manage Sessions** button, and a **Privacy Policy** link that opens your externally hosted policy page. See [Settings](../help/settings.md) and [Sessions](sessions.md).

## What is on the screen

Two accordion groups.

**Privacy Policy**

- Information We Collect
- How We Use Information
- Information Sharing
- Data Retention
- Your Rights

**Safety Guidelines**

- Helmet Safety
- Traffic Rules
- Vehicle Inspection
- Weather Awareness
- Stay Alert
- Parking Rules

That is the whole screen today: two reading sections and no action buttons. This is the expected state.

## Data-management buttons

**Download My Data** and **Request Account Deletion** are not currently available in the app.

- Do not tell a rider they can export their data from this screen. A formal data-access request has to be handled by your team outside the app
- Do not send a rider to **Request Account Deletion** here. The working path is **Profile → Delete Account** ([Profile](profile.md)), which schedules the deletion and offers a cancel window

## Are the safety guidelines enforceable rules?

No — they are guidance shown to the rider. The rules that are actually enforced come from your own configuration:

- Where a ride may end — see [Zones](../../settings/infrastructure/zones.md)
- What a ride costs and the minimum balance to start — see [Vehicle Tariffs](../../settings/infrastructure/vehicle-tariffs.md)
- Parking photo requirements — see [Rides](../riding/rides.md)

## Where the legal documents live

- The policy **text** shown in the app is the accordion content on this screen
- **Settings** additionally carries a **Privacy Policy** link that opens your externally hosted page, when one is configured
- You set the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Licenses links in **Settings → My Company → App → Legal & compliance** ([My Company](../../settings/administration/my-company.md))
- Reference copies: [Terms of Service](../../legal/policies/terms-of-service.md) and [Privacy Policy](../../legal/policies/privacy-policy.md)

## FAQ

- **How does a rider actually delete their account?** **Profile → Delete Account**. It schedules the deletion and can be cancelled while the cancel window is open.
- **Can a rider download their data from the app?** No — not currently available in the app.
- **The Request Account Deletion button does nothing.** It is not currently available in the app. Use the Profile flow.
- **Where is the Privacy entry in the menu?** In the map side menu. There is no bottom navigation in this app.
- **The screen has no buttons at all.** Expected — the two reading sections are the whole screen.
