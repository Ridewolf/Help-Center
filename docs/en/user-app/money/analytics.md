# Rider App — Rider Statistics

**Rider-facing statistics are not currently available in the app.** There is no charts screen, no totals, and no spend summary for a rider to open.

Send riders to [History](history.md) instead — it is the only place with their own data.

## What a rider sees

- **There is no entry point.** The side drawer lists Wallet, History, Support, Privacy, Settings and Profile — and nothing else. A rider who says they cannot find an analytics screen is right; do not tell them to look in the menu, and do not send them a link to one.
- If the `/analytics` screen is opened directly, it shows only a heading and empty space. **Nothing is wrong** with the rider's account, their device, or the app install — a reinstall changes nothing.

## Where the rider's numbers actually live

[History](history.md) has real per-rider data:

- The **Rides** tab lists every past ride with its distance, duration and cost
- The **Payments** tab lists top-ups, refunds, debits and bonuses with amounts and statuses
- Tapping a ride opens its detail with the full [cost breakdown](../riding/rides.md#cost-breakdown), the activity timeline, and the route drawn on a map

There is **no aggregate totals banner anywhere** in the rider app — not on any statistics screen, and not at the top of History. Lifetime totals have to be added up from the ride list, or pulled from your own reporting.

## Answering the numbers questions from the dashboard

When a rider genuinely needs totals, produce them on the operator side:

| Rider wants                     | Where you get it                                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Total spend over a period       | [Analytics — Payments](../../analytics/reports/payments.md)                        |
| Their own ride list, exported   | [Rides — List](../../operations/trips/rides.md), filtered to that client           |
| Their payment records           | [Payments — History](../../operations/payments/payments.md)                        |
| A quick per-client summary      | [Client Detail](../../operations/customers/client-detail.md) — lifetime ride count, balance, rating |

## FAQ

| Rider asks…                     | Answer                                                                          |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Where are my stats?"           | Not currently available in the app. Use [History](history.md)                    |
| "I can't find Analytics in the menu" | There is no menu entry for it                                               |
| "The Analytics page is blank"   | Expected — the screen is not currently available. Nothing is broken              |
| "Can I export my ride data?"    | Not from the app. Export it from the dashboard on the rider's behalf             |
| "How much have I spent in total?" | No total exists in the rider app. Read it off History, or pull it from the dashboard |

## Tips

- **Do not send analytics links to riders.** There is no screen worth landing on, and a blank page reads as a broken app.
- **Answer totals questions yourself.** Pulling the figure from the dashboard takes a minute and ends the conversation.
- **History is the honest answer**, and it is genuinely complete per ride and per payment — frame it that way rather than apologising for a missing screen.
