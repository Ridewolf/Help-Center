# Ridewolf Help Center

Official documentation and community support for the **Ridewolf platform** for shared-mobility fleets: the operator Dashboard, the Rider App, and the field-operator Service App.

English, Russian and Romanian are written by the product team. The other languages are translated from English automatically and reviewed over time — if a wording is off, [report it](https://github.com/Ridewolf/Help-Center/issues/new?template=documentation.yml) or open a pull request. Where a translation and the English article disagree, English is correct.

The Help Center covers fleet operations, rides, customers, payments, support workflows, analytics, dashboard settings, and everyday product features — plus guides for the Rider App (`user-app`), the field-operator Service App (`service-app`), and the [Terms of Service and Privacy Policy](docs/en/legal/policies/terms-of-service.md) (`legal`; English is the binding version).

## Support

- [Ask a product question](https://github.com/Ridewolf/Help-Center/issues/new?template=support.yml)
- [Report unclear or outdated documentation](https://github.com/Ridewolf/Help-Center/issues/new?template=documentation.yml)
- [Read the support and privacy guidance](SUPPORT.md)
- [Report a security vulnerability privately](https://github.com/Ridewolf/Help-Center/security/advisories/new)

> This repository is public. Never include passwords, API keys, payment information, customer data, or other confidential information in an issue.
